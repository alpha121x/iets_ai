AI COACH — FREE TOOLS AND RELIABLE DATA PLAN

Yes—an accurate MVP can use mostly free tools. However, a fully global,
always-current university database cannot be maintained reliably with free AI only.

FREE COMPONENTS

1. Transcript extraction
   - Use PyMuPDF and/or Tesseract OCR.
   - Student must confirm extracted CGPA, degree, subjects, and graduation year.

2. University matching
   - Use PostgreSQL and rule-based matching logic.
   - Do not allow an AI model to invent eligibility requirements.

3. Chatbot and test preparation
   - Free local models are possible, but quality will be lower.
   - Paid API models provide stronger IELTS and international-test coaching.

4. University, test, and scholarship data
   - Use official sources only.
   - Store the official source URL and the date when each record was verified.

RELIABILITY RULES

Every recommendation should show:

- Required IELTS, GRE, GMAT, SAT, or other test scores
- Scholarship availability, eligibility, and deadline
- Official university or scholarship link
- Last verified date
- Clear status: Eligible, Possible Match, or Verify with University

Never guarantee admission or scholarship approval.

RECOMMENDED LAUNCH SCOPE

Start with 50–100 manually verified UK programmes, then add Germany.
Do not claim worldwide coverage in the first version.

OFFICIAL FREE SOURCES

- UK Discover Uni / HESA: official UK course and higher-education data
  https://www.discoveruni.gov.uk/about-our-data/

- DAAD Scholarship Database: official German scholarship information for
  international students and researchers
  https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/

IMPORTANT

Admissions requirements, English-language requirements, test rules,
scholarships, tuition fees, and deadlines can change. The platform must use
structured data with a source URL and periodic verification workflow. AI should
explain the match; the database should determine the eligibility result.
