AI COACH — CURRENT DATA, SOURCES, AND UI AUDIT

1. CURRENT DATA USED IN THE PROTOTYPE

Student data (stored in PostgreSQL)
- Full name and email
- Target country and programme/field
- CGPA
- IELTS Reading, Listening, Writing, Speaking, and overall scores
- Writing submissions and practice attempts
- Uploaded transcript file name, extracted PDF text, and detected CGPA

How transcript data works
- The backend accepts a PDF transcript.
- PyMuPDF extracts readable text from the PDF.
- The system looks for CGPA/GPA patterns in that text.
- The student must confirm the extracted CGPA, degree, subjects, and other
  academic details. Extraction alone must never be treated as final proof.

University opportunity data (stored in PostgreSQL)
- University and programme name
- Country, city, website, degree, and field
- Minimum IELTS and section requirements
- Minimum CGPA
- Tuition fee and application deadline
- Required tests, for example IELTS, GRE, GMAT, SAT
- Scholarship name, amount, eligibility, deadline, and source URL
- Official source URL and last-verified date fields

IMPORTANT CURRENT STATUS
- The current three universities/programmes and scholarship/test entries are
  demonstration seed data for the prototype.
- They are NOT a production-ready or complete university database.
- The client-facing product must label all data with its source and last
  verification date.

2. RELIABLE PRODUCTION DATA SOURCES

Primary source: official university pages
- Programme admissions/entry-requirements page
- International student English-language requirements page
- Tuition-fee page
- Scholarship/funding page
- Official application deadline page

Secondary official sources
- UK Discover Uni / HESA: course and higher-education statistics
  https://www.discoveruni.gov.uk/about-our-data/
- DAAD Scholarship Database: German scholarships for international students
  https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/

Data rule
- The system should not scrape or copy unverified information into eligibility
  decisions.
- Each programme requirement must have: source URL, verified date, reviewer,
  and verification status.
- The matching engine is rule-based; AI explains a result but does not invent
  requirements or guarantee admission/scholarships.

5. PRIVACY AND ACCURACY

- Transcripts are sensitive personal data: restrict access to the student and
  authorised staff only.
- Do not expose transcript text through public APIs.
- Use HTTPS, encrypted backups, deletion controls, and an explicit privacy
  policy before release.
- Show "verify with the university" for any data that is old, incomplete, or
  not directly sourced from an official page.
